What the New Global Order (Might) Look Like

Oct 18, 2026 · 23 min · 42 sources

"The architect is gone. What does the next world order look like?"

September 3rd, 2025. Beijing.

Xi Jinping stands above Tiananmen Square, watching tanks and missile launchers roll past in formation. Next to him is Vladimir Putin. A few steps away is Kim Jong Un. It's the largest Chinese military parade in years, marking eighty years since Japan's surrender.

Two days earlier, in the northern city of Tianjin, Xi hosted something harder to ignore. Leaders from more than twenty countries showed up for the Shanghai Cooperation Organization summit; Russia, Iran, North Korea, and, for the first time in years, India. Prime Minister Narendra Modi flew in personally.

Onstage, Xi calls for an end to what he calls "hegemonism and power politics." He talks about "true multilateralism." He launches something called the Global Governance Initiative.

He never says the word America. He doesn't have to.

While Washington was drafting a strategy document about stepping back from the world, Beijing was busy building for what comes after.

Let's look at what comes next.
